Rules

  • $100 buy-in per person
  • Bets must come from FanDuel, DraftKings, or Oddshark
  • Submit your pick Tuesday through Monday — no submission means an automatic loss for the week
  • Can't take the same line as someone else — first come, first served. Check This Week's Picks to see what's already taken
  • Odds must be −120 or higher
  • Player props are eligible
  • One POTY, required at some point this season — win = 2 wins, loss = 2 losses
  • One DOTD, optional — a +200-or-longer underdog; win = 2 wins, loss = 1 loss
  • Season Long pick required, −140 or higher — counts as a win once it's settled. Use the Submit Season Long Pick button below
  • Ties are settled in the playoffs
